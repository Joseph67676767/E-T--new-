About John Cena

John Cena is an actor from the USA.

He was a wrestler in the past.

He recorded 17 times as a world champion when he was a wrestler.

Fun Facts about John Cena

  • Halloween Save: His famous "Doctor of Thuganomics" rapping persona started by accident when he dressed as Vanilla Ice for a backstage Halloween party and freestyled for WWE staff.
  • He spent years learning Mandarin fluently so he could promote WWE events directly to fans in China.
  • His 2005 rap album, You Can't See Me, debuted at No. 15 on the US Billboard 200 chart and went platinum-level certified in sales.
